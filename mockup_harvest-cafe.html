Your Go-To Spot on George Street

Harvest Cafe has been a fixture at 218 George Street for years, and it's the kind of place that feels like home the moment you walk in. Shona and the team have built something special here — a proper Kiwi cafe where the food is fresh, the coffee is consistently excellent, and every customer gets a genuine welcome. Regulars meet here weekly. Visitors make it a must-stop every time they're back in Dunedin. That says everything.

We believe good food doesn't need to be complicated — it just needs to be done right. Fresh scones straight from the oven, pinwheel sandwiches made daily, lamingtons that people rave about, and cheese rolls that have earned a loyal following. Add perfectly made coffee and a team that genuinely loves what they do, and you've got Harvest. We're proud to be part of the George Street community, and we can't wait to welcome you in.

Why Choose Harvest Cafe

  • Legendary cheese rolls — Shona's recipe brings people back from across the city and beyond
  • Consistently excellent coffee — smooth, strong, and exactly how you like it
  • Fresh scones baked throughout the day — still warm, perfectly fluffy, worth arriving early for
  • Real Kiwi classics done right — pinwheel sandwiches, lamingtons, and salmon eggs benedict that regulars rave about
  • Genuine welcome every time — staff who remember your order and make you feel at home
  • Central George Street location — easy to find, wheelchair accessible, perfect for a quick coffee or leisurely lunch
