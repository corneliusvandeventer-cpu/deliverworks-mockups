A Quirky George Street Icon

Kiki Beware isn't your typical Dunedin cafe — and that's exactly how we like it. Tucked into George Street in the heart of Central Dunedin, we've built a loyal following around bold flavours, recycled-door tables, and an atmosphere that feels more like hanging out with mates than dining out. Ranked #96 of 332 restaurants in Dunedin on TripAdvisor with 254 reviews, we're proof that doing things differently works.

We're here for the coffee lovers, the fries fanatics, the vegans and the meat eaters, the students pulling all-nighters and the locals who just want a damn good feed. Our vibe is welcoming, our menu is unapologetically flavourful, and our team genuinely love what they do. Come as you are — quirks, cravings, and all.
