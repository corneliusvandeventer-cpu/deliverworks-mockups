George Street's Quirky Boutique Cafe

Kiki Beware has carved out its own little corner of Central Dunedin with a vibe that's equal parts welcoming and wonderfully weird. Nestled on George Street, we're the spot with the recycled-door tables, the loyal regulars, and the kind of atmosphere that makes you want to linger just a little longer. Ranked #96 of 332 restaurants in Dunedin on TripAdvisor with 254 reviews, we've built a reputation on doing things our way — and people love us for it.

We're all about big flavours in a boutique setting. From our signature deconstructed Mexican iced coffee to our legendary Holly fries, everything we serve is crafted with care and a little bit of creative flair. Our team is chilled, our vibe is genuine, and whether you're a regular or a first-timer, you'll feel right at home the moment you walk through the door.
