Your Hamilton East Mechanics Since Day One

Midnight Motors sits tucked behind the Z Station on the corner of Grey St and Naylor St in Hamilton East. Rodney and Jason have built this MTA approved workshop on one simple principle: tell people the truth, charge them fairly, and do the job properly. Over the years, that straightforward approach has earned them a loyal following of customers who are sick of being taken for a ride by other garages.

We're not fancy and we're not corporate — we're just two mechanics who love what we do and respect the people who trust us with their cars. Same-day repairs when we can manage it, honest diagnostics every time, and fair pricing that won't make you wince. That's how we've built a reputation strong enough that entire families now bring their cars to us.
